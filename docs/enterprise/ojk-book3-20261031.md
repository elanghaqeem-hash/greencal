## Key implementation rules

- The main financed emissions figure is the attributed Scope 1 plus Scope 2, and is **null/incomplete** if either component is missing. It never silently becomes zero.
- Scope 3 is separately returned as `supplementaryFinancedScope3Tco2e`; never add it to `financedTco2e` main figure or operational Scope 1/2 of the bank.
- The debtors' Scope 1 is the default **carbon cost base**; full debtor emissions, not the bank's financing share. Enter `reductionFraction` from a sourced scenario, not an invented factor.
- Tier 1 and 2 require borrower-reported evidence and specific distinction/verification against **Buku 2**. Tier 3 activity calculator estimate; Tier 4 peer proxy supported only if sector, business model, scaling basis and evidence are supplied.
- PCAF data-quality scores 1–5 are additional; they are **never auto-mapped** to OJK Tier 1–4.
- Asset-class attribution documented in §3.2: business loans (equity + debt); listed equity/corporate bonds (EVIC or suitable equity+debt); CRE (property value); project finance (project equity + debt). Other PCAF asset classes in `quant.js` require a distinct approved method and do not inherit a fabricated Buku 3 denominator.
- Decimal arithmetic uses integer-scaled BigInt with **one final rounding** for each attributed scope, instead of rounding the attribution ratio prematurely.
- Faktor emisi dari tahun lain, satuan tidak sama, dan missing values ditolak; faktor aktual **tidak ditanamkan** dalam kode.

## Reproduction: hypothetical Buku 3 pp. 31–32

PT ABC published Scope 1 = 885,087; Scope 2 = 23,583; Scope 3 = 686,906 tCO2e. Outstanding IDR 500,000,000,000, total equity + debt IDR 12,000,000,000,000; exact share 1/24 or about 4.17%.

- Main financed S1+S2: 908,670 / 24 = **37,861.25 tCO2e** (the guide rounds to ~37,861).
- Supplementary S3: 686,906 / 24 = **28,621.083333 tCO2e**.
- Full debtor Scope 1 emission gap 20% = **177,017.4 tCO2e**.
- At IDR 800,000/tCO2e, debtor carbon cost = **IDR 141,613,920,000** (~IDR 141,614 million).
- At debtor revenue IDR 8,000,000 million, intensity = **1.770174%** (~1.77%).
- All example amounts are **hypothetical**, not emission factors or bank data.

## API & UI

- Menu: Enterprise → **Buku 3 · Metode Penghitungan Emisi**.
- `GET /api/enterprise/book3/catalog`: requirements and methods.
- `POST /api/enterprise/book3/validate`: `{"kind":"debtor|financed|carbon_cost|activity","payload":{...}}`.
- Gate: `GC_ENTERPRISE_BOOK3_ENABLED=true` **only in a controlled, bank-authorized environment**; default false.
- Protected by parent `handleEnterpriseApi`: identity/tenant membership/same-origin/role validation. No cross-tenant cache and no browser persistent data.
- Results are previews only; no new DB tables, backend data import or regulator submission. Existing `/analytics/import` continues to store tenant-scoped immutable reconciliation batches and reports a separate Scope 3.
- On Vercel, the new feature flag must be explicitly passed through `api/bridge.js`.

1. Obtain the OJK **Annex C Excel emission calculator**, validate each factor, vintage, conversion and spreadsheet calculation, and compare to imported activity/Scope data and independent samples.
2. Confirm organization/operational boundaries, actual debtor Scope 1–3 report/proxies, assurance level and OJK Tier methodology against Buku 1–2.
3. Validate CBS/GL/CKPN portfolio totals, financing share denominators, outstanding by date, FX, class mapping, securities and Islamic finance treatment, avoiding double counting.
4. Complete approved Buku 4 emission pathways, Buku 2 CRSA earnings/PD/LGD/EAD links, Buku 6 reconciliation, approved Buku 7 scenario engine as applicable.
5. Complete SSO tenant binding, PostgreSQL environment, audit trails, backup/restore, cybersecurity testing, UAT, independent model validation, Risk/Finance/Compliance/TI authorizations.
6. Obtain written approval before enabling the flag or labelling the application production-ready for confidential bank data.

## Additional precision/provenance review — 31 October 2026

- Preserve the actual Tier 4 comparator sector, comparator business model, scaling basis and scaling evidence in the **result's debtor source snapshot**, not just in validated request inputs. This helps auditors trace peer-proxy estimates (PDF p. 33).
- Report both outstanding and attributed denominator, basis and currency alongside the calculated share. Never silently substitute a loan balance for the equity/debt, EVIC, property-value or project-capital denominator.
- In activity calculations, multiply activity quantity × factor in exact scaled integers and convert kgCO2e to tCO2e with **one final rounding**. Do not first round kilogram values before division by 1,000.
- For carbon costs, price the unrounded emission gap, while separately displaying the gap rounded for reporting; this prevents intermediate rounding drift in very small exposures.
- Source PDF Annex C (PDF p. 47) states the linked Excel workbook is integral to the guide. Its QR code points to an OJK SharePoint folder, but the workbook itself was **not provided or independently retrieved**. The engine must not claim to reproduce the official spreadsheet or factor library until formulas/factors are version-by-version reconciled.
- Automated regression cases for Tier 4 lineage, denominators, final-rounding activity conversion and unrounded-gap pricing are in `tests/book3.test.mjs`.

This control matrix represents **code mapping**, not regulatory conformance certification. Independent Bank Risk, Finance, Compliance and quantitative-model sign-off is outstanding.
