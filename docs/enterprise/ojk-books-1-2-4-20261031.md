## Book 4 data-entry contract
A single UTF-8 CSV per variable with header: year,scenario,value

Exactly 104 rows: 2025–2050 inclusive for Reference, Current Policies, Delayed Transition and Net Zero 2050. One observation per year/path. Numeric strings with at most 6 fractional places. Unit, original PDF page, and uploaded source fingerprint are mandatory.

Reference is comparison-only and MUST NOT appear as a reported CRSA climate scenario. Deviations always compare the same year. Reconciled book-level source hash is NOT equivalent to the validation of individual rows from image/raster tables. The workbench NEVER claims the input data are officially verified.

## End-to-end dependency
Governance / Discovery → approved OJK source registry → bank portfolio + debtor emission inputs + physical datasets → validated Book 4 macro vintage → Book 2 risk transmission and Book 7 standardized calculation → independent model validation → Buku 6 reconciliation → maker/checker/approver → authorized internal or regulator report.

Not implemented in this increment: transcription of every official Book 4 numerical table; database persistence and bank approval of macro series; Book 2 full cash-flow-to-PD/LGD/EAD recalibration; real CBS/GL/CKPN/NGFS/GIS ingestion; official regulatory release. Existing modules may cover part of these but cannot be assumed accepted end-to-end without evidence.
